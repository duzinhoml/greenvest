GreenVest.

Rental Property Sustainability Simulator

REE 2610 · Sustainable Real Estate · Florida Gulf Coast University

Who it serves & the problem it solves

Southwest Florida rental investors and property managers need to know whether sustainability upgrades benefit the owner, tenants, or both. GreenVest compares solar, HVAC and window alternatives using transparent income and energy assumptions.

Three course topics, three working tools

01 · The Physics Bill Comes DueCalculate avoided electricity costs; allocate benefits by owner, tenant or shared bill payment.
04 · Gulfview CourtBuild potential rental income, effective gross income, annual expenses and baseline/projected NOI.
12 · Sustainability PaysCompare net cost, owner and tenant savings, positive owner payback and income-based value change.

Inputs & calculations

Total monthly rent, vacancy, annual expense categories, cap rate, contractor cost, avoided kWh, electricity rate, maintenance and documented benefits. NOI = effective income − expenses. Owner payback = net cost ÷ positive owner savings. Value = NOI ÷ cap rate.

Outputs & originality

Financial dashboard, interactive benefit charts, five rankings and exported analysis. The split-incentive model prevents tenant utility savings from inflating landlord NOI. Independent alternatives avoid double-counting. Reusable for future acquisition and retrofit screening.

Worked example · 5529 6th Avenue, Fort Myers

Preliminary duplex rent: $1,750 × 2 × 12 = $42,000 potential annual income. Assume 5% vacancy and $12,000 annual expenses: effective income is $39,900 and NOI is $27,900. These vacancy and expense values are illustrative, not actual records.

Assume a $10,000 upgrade saves 3,000 kWh/year at $0.1524/kWh, with $100 additional annual owner maintenance and no documented incentives or insurance benefits. Tenant-paid electricity yields $457.20 tenant savings, −$100 owner savings, $27,800 projected NOI and no positive owner payback. At an assumed 6% cap rate, estimated value change is −$1,666.67. Owner-paid electricity instead yields $357.20 owner savings and 28.0-year payback. Neither scenario establishes investment suitability.

Evidence & practical limits

MLS #226033963: $280,000 asking, 1,344 sq ft, built 1971; 2025 tax $3,417.07; tenant electricity, roof and A/C claims. Listing figures are unaudited; conflicting $38,400 NOI is excluded. NREL PVWatts, DOE and ENERGY STAR are research references; no property savings or costs are fabricated. Unknown expenses and upgrade estimates remain blank. Value estimates are scenarios, not appraisals.
